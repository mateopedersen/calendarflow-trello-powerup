CalendarFlow Support

CalendarFlow is a read-only Trello Power-Up by Beta Calendars.

Getting help

For setup questions or a problem with the planner, use the Beta Calendars contact page and include “CalendarFlow” in your message. Do not include private board names, card content, or access tokens in a support request.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Enable CalendarFlow on the board, then choose Printable Calendar in its toolbar.
  • If cards do not load, reopen the planner from that board. Archived cards and cards in archived lists are excluded by Trello's visible-card method.
  • If a preference appears stale, clear site data for the connector and choose the week start again.
  • CSV and ICS files download through the browser. Check its downloads list if needed.
  • For a PDF, choose Print / Save PDF and select your browser's PDF destination.

Data and permissions

See the Privacy Notice. CalendarFlow does not modify Trello cards or send board contents to Beta Calendars.
