# CalendarFlow — Printable Project Planner

Turn dates already on your Trello board into a clear plan you can use on screen or on paper. CalendarFlow reads visible cards on the current board and gives you focused calendar views, practical deadline counts, and exports created in your browser.

![CalendarFlow monthly planner preview using synthetic sample cards](https://mateopedersen.github.io/calendarflow-trello-powerup/docs/assets/calendarflow-month-preview.png)

## Plan by month, week, or year

- **Month:** See card start and due dates, labels, completion state, overdue markers, and links back to Trello cards. Select a day to review its cards.
- **Week:** Focus on a seven-day schedule. Choose Monday-first or Sunday-first weeks.
- **Year:** Scan a compact twelve-month overview with deadline counts.
- **Insights:** Count visible cards with due dates, completed due-date cards, overdue incomplete cards, upcoming incomplete cards, and cards without a due date. The monthly density view shows dates with more deadlines; it does not estimate effort.

![CalendarFlow deadline insights preview using synthetic sample cards](https://mateopedersen.github.io/calendarflow-trello-powerup/docs/assets/calendarflow-insights-preview.png)

## Print and export on your device

Choose A4 or US Letter paper, portrait or landscape orientation, card titles on or off, and monochrome printing. CalendarFlow opens your browser's print dialog; it does not create or send a PDF through an external service. CSV and iCalendar (ICS) files are generated locally when you choose an export.

## Simple to try

No CalendarFlow account or subscription is required for the initial release. Enable CalendarFlow on a Trello board and choose **Printable Calendar**. [Open the interactive sample planner](https://mateopedersen.github.io/calendarflow-trello-powerup/src/planner.html?demo=1) to explore clearly labeled example data before enabling it on a board.

CalendarFlow reads visible cards and open lists from the current board through Trello's Power-Up Client Library. It does not edit Trello cards or send board content to Beta Calendars. Optional printable resources open only when selected and do not include board details.
