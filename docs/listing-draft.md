**Description**

# CalendarFlow — Printable Project Planner

See the dates already on your Trello board in a monthly calendar, a seven-day planner, and a compact year overview. Review due-date counts, completion status and overdue cards, then print a paper layout or export deadlines to CSV or iCalendar.

CalendarFlow reads visible cards from the current board in your browser. It does not edit cards or send board details to Beta Calendars. Printing uses your browser's print dialog; CSV and ICS files are generated locally.

The optional resource panel links to printable templates from [Beta Calendars](https://www.betacalendars.com/). External pages open only after you select them.

Developed by [Beta Calendars](https://www.betacalendars.com/).
