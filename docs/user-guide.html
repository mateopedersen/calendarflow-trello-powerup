CalendarFlow Quick Guide

  1. Enable CalendarFlow on a Trello board.
  2. Click Printable Calendar in the board toolbar.
  3. Switch among Month, Week, Year, Insights, and Resources.
  4. Choose Monday or Sunday as the week start. Use the arrows and Today control to navigate.
  5. Set paper size, orientation, card-title visibility, and monochrome printing under Print settings.
  6. Choose Print / Save PDF to use your browser's print dialog.
  7. Use CSV or ICS to download board deadlines to this device.

Calendar and analytics

Dates with a start or due date appear in the month cells. Clicking a day shows related cards. Card names link to Trello when a card URL is available. Deadline analytics count cards, not hours or effort. Overdue means an incomplete due date falls before today's local calendar date; upcoming means an incomplete due date is today or later.

Resources

Optional Beta Calendars resources open only when clicked. CalendarFlow does not add Trello card details to those links.
